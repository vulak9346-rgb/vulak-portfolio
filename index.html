Workflow

How I usually do my models

Idea & Concept definition

You give me the idea, the format, and what it's for, and I'll adjust it to your needs as much as possible.

Model sketch

Once the idea is defined, I'll make you a simple sketch of the model and its main structure, and you can tell me what you think.

Final details

Once you give me your feedback, I'll finalize the details and you'll have your model ready to add to your project.
